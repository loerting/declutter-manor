# Third-party assets

Every texture in this project is **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication): free for
commercial use, modification and redistribution, **with no attribution required**. The credits
below are given voluntarily, as a courtesy to the people who made the scans and so that any
future licence question can be answered without re-deriving where a file came from.

Nothing here imposes an obligation on a shipped build. There is no notice we are required to
include, and no source we must link back to.

The files themselves are not committed (they are ~90 MB and freely re-downloadable). Fetch them
with `python3 tools/fetch_textures.py`, which reads `assets/textures.json`.

## The brief the scans were chosen against

The house is a suburban American centre-hall colonial of the 2000s, and every surface was picked
for what that house is actually built and finished with, not for what looks best on a sphere:
vinyl lap siding (the most used exterior wall material on new US homes, 27.7% of 2025 starts,
Census Survey of Construction via NAHB), architectural asphalt shingles (three roofs in four),
oak strip in the living areas, porcelain tile in the baths and mudroom, plush carpet in the
bedrooms, a steel-trowelled slab in the garage and basement, a broom-finished driveway, and
granite over white shaker cabinets. Every candidate was judged from ambientCG's sphere render
and its flat colour map side by side, and the winners again in the game (`dev/HouseView.tscn`).

## Scans considered and rejected for this house

* **RoofingTiles013A** (the roof until 2026-09-18) and every other ambientCG / Poly Haven roof:
  clay, concrete tile, slate and thatch. Clay reads Mediterranean; even slate (`roof_slates_03`,
  RoofingTiles003) reads upscale New England, not a suburban asphalt roof.
* **wood_floor** (Poly Haven, the floor until 2026-09-18): wide dark rustic planks.
* **Marble012** (worktops and the kitchen floor until 2026-09-18): grey marble slab, and as a
  floor it read as a stone hall. Kitchens here are floored in the hall's oak.
* **PaintedWood00x**: every painted-wood scan is cracked, weathered boards; interior trim and
  cabinet fronts are smooth.
* **Plaster00x, PaintedPlaster00x** for the walls: trowelled Mediterranean plaster. Painted
  drywall is nearly uniform, which the Poly Haven wall already is.
* **Concrete040** (every slab and paving until 2026-09-18): a rough brown exposed aggregate that
  read as carpet in the garage.
* **Grass004**, **Gravel023**: the lawn read olive and oversaturated; the gravel was white enough
  to read as concrete from the air.
* **GlazedTerracotta001**: a flowerpot is not glazed.
* **Fabric020** for towels: a woven check, not a terry loop; on a towel it rendered as a grey
  mosaic. The berber loop at a third of its size reads closer to terry.
* **Rope001/003**: the strands are diagonal, and triplanar mapping cannot make them follow a rope.
* **Tiles132A / Tiles020** pool mosaics: a European pool.
* **Bricks075A, Bricks084, Bricks060** for the firebox: stone and whitewash.

## Sources deliberately not used

These were on the shortlist and were rejected, so that nobody re-adds them later without
knowing why:

* **Textures.com** — the free tier is credit-based and its licence does **not** cleanly permit
  redistribution inside a commercial game. Avoid.
* **FreePBR** — "free for commercial use", but not CC0; the terms restrict redistribution of the
  raw texture files. Usable, but it drags a per-asset licence question along with it.
* **ShareTextures**, **CG Bookcase**, **Texture Can** — genuinely CC0 and fine to use. Skipped
  only because Poly Haven and ambientCG already covered every surface and both expose an open
  API, so the whole set can be re-fetched by script.
* **3DAssets.one** — a meta-search over the above, not a source of its own.

## Everything else

All geometry is generated at runtime by `props/Props.gd` and `world/HouseBuilder.gd`. There are no imported models,
sounds, or fonts, and no other third-party content in this repository.
