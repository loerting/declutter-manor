When a design choice trades player effort against purity or literalness, pick the convenient one
(e.g. pointing at a home auto-selects the matching carried item instead of scrolling to it).

**Why:** the author (2026-09-16) called convenience "one general attribute of the game that has to
always be done as best as possible".

**How to apply:** in every interaction design, remove steps the game can infer; recommend the
convenient option first. Related: [[game-vision-declutter-manor]].
